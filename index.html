About Me

I am a third-year DEM student at the University of Toronto Mississauga with a strong interest in marketing and digital marketing. I was born and raised in Tainan, Taiwan, and I hold both Canadian and Taiwanese citizenship. Tainan is the oldest city in Taiwan and is especially famous for its history, culture, and food. I may be a little biased, but I genuinely think it has some of the best food in Taiwan. Outside of school, music is a big part of my life. I really enjoy listening to music and going to concerts, especially the experience of seeing artists perform live. I also love skiing and enjoy spending time in the ski resorts with my friends whenever I get the chance.
